      <h2>Introduction</h2>
      <p>
        Singleton SD ("we," "our," or "us") operates Platform Kit available at
        <a href="https://plattform-kit.poc.singletonsd.com">plattform-kit.poc.singletonsd.com</a>
        and
        <a href="https://app.plattform-kit.poc.singletonsd.com"
          >app.plattform-kit.poc.singletonsd.com</a
        >
        (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard
        your information when you use our Service.
      </p>

      <h2>Information We Collect</h2>
      <h3>Account information</h3>
      <p>When you sign in, we may collect:</p>
      <ul>
        <li>Name and email address from Microsoft Entra ID</li>
        <li>Tenant or organisation identifiers associated with your account</li>
        <li>Role and session claims used for authentication and coarse authorisation</li>
      </ul>

      <h3>Service data</h3>
      <p>We store content and records you create or manage through the Service, such as:</p>
      <ul>
        <li>Tenant, contact, support, subscription, and related pillar records</li>
        <li>Audit and reporting events generated by Service activity</li>
        <li>Support interactions and operational notes</li>
      </ul>

      <h3>Usage and diagnostic data</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>Application logs, feature usage, and errors</li>
        <li>
          Device and browser information (type, OS, approximate location at city/country level)
        </li>
        <li>IP address, session timing, and performance/diagnostic signals</li>
      </ul>

      <h2>How We Use Your Information</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>Provide, maintain, and secure the Service</li>
        <li>Authenticate users and enforce tenancy / access controls</li>
        <li>Send transactional notifications (email, SMS, or WhatsApp where configured)</li>
        <li>Detect abuse, debug issues, and improve reliability</li>
        <li>Comply with legal obligations</li>
      </ul>

      <h2>Data Storage and Security</h2>
      <p>
        Your data is hosted on Microsoft Azure in Australia East. Secrets are stored in Azure Key
        Vault. We use TLS in transit, encryption at rest where provided by Azure services,
        role-based access controls, and managed identities for cloud access.
      </p>
      <p>
        If a personal-data breach occurs, we will notify affected users and relevant authorities as
        required by applicable law.
      </p>

      <h2>Data Sharing</h2>
      <p>We do not sell your personal information. We may share data:</p>
      <ul>
        <li>With your organisation's administrators for tenant management</li>
        <li>With service providers listed above, solely to operate the Service</li>
        <li>When required by law, subpoena, or court order</li>
        <li>In connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset transfer (with notice)</li>
      </ul>

      <h2>Data Retention</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>
          Account and service data: for the life of the account, then deleted or anonymised within
          about 30 days after deletion (unless law requires longer retention)
        </li>
        <li>Usage logs: typically up to 12 months</li>
        <li>Application Insights / Log Analytics telemetry: 30 days (workspace default)</li>
        <li>Support records: typically up to 3 years</li>
      </ul>

      <h2>Your Rights</h2>
      <p>
        Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, or export of your
        personal data, and object to certain processing. Contact us at the email below to exercise
        these rights.
      </p>

      <h2>Cookies</h2>
      <p>
        For this PoC we use cookies and similar technologies primarily for authentication and
        session continuity. You can control cookies through your browser settings; blocking them may
        prevent sign-in.
      </p>

      <h2>International Data Transfers</h2>
      <p>
        The Service is hosted in Australia. If you access it from elsewhere, your data may be
        transferred to and processed in Australia. We rely on appropriate contractual and technical
        safeguards with sub-processors where required.
      </p>

      <h2>Children's Privacy</h2>
      <p>
        The Service is not intended for users under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal
        information from children under 16.
      </p>
