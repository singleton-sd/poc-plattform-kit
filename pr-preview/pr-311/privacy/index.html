Notice to Platform Operators

This is a PoC sample policy adapted from an open MIT SaaS template. It is not lawyer-reviewed legal advice and may be replaced with counsel-approved terms later.

1. Introduction

Singleton SD (“we,” “our,” or “us”) operates Platform Kit available at plattform-kit.poc.singletonsd.com and app.plattform-kit.poc.singletonsd.com (the “Service”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Service.

2. Information We Collect

Account information

When you sign in, we may collect:

  1. Name and email address from Microsoft Entra ID
  2. Tenant or organisation identifiers associated with your account
  3. Role and session claims used for authentication and coarse authorisation

Service data

We store content and records you create or manage through the Service, such as:

  1. Tenant, contact, support, subscription, and related pillar records
  2. Audit and reporting events generated by Service activity
  3. Support interactions and operational notes

Marketing contact and waitlist

When you submit the public contact or waitlist form on plattform-kit.poc.singletonsd.com, we collect:

  1. Name, email address, and optional company and phone
  2. Your message (contact) or the waitlist feature you selected
  3. Required privacy consent and optional marketing opt-in
  4. A Cloudflare Turnstile token used only to confirm the submission is human. Cloudflare also processes the visitor IP address, TLS fingerprint, User-Agent, site key, and origin. The token is verified server-side and is not stored after that check. See Cloudflare’s Turnstile Privacy Addendum.
  5. Source site and page (https://plattform-kit.poc.singletonsd.com and the form path)

Consent is recorded as version platform-kit-privacy-v1. Submissions are posted from your browser to Singleton SD’s shared lead-intake API and stored in our CRM (Twenty) as a Platform Kit (platform_kit) lead so we can reply or email you about waitlist features you asked for.

If JavaScript is unavailable, the forms cannot be submitted; email support@singletonsd.com instead.

Usage and diagnostic data

  1. Application logs, feature usage, and errors
  2. Device and browser information (type, OS, approximate location at city/country level)
  3. IP address, session timing, and performance/diagnostic signals

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, maintain, and secure the Service
  • Authenticate users and enforce tenancy / access controls
  • Send transactional notifications (email, SMS, or WhatsApp where configured)
  • Detect abuse, debug issues, and improve reliability
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is hosted on Microsoft Azure in Australia East. Secrets are stored in Azure Key Vault. We use TLS in transit, encryption at rest where provided by Azure services, role-based access controls, and managed identities for cloud access.

If a personal-data breach occurs, we will notify affected users and relevant authorities as required by applicable law.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data:

  • With your organisation’s administrators for tenant management
  • With service providers listed above, solely to operate the Service
  • When required by law, subpoena, or court order
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset transfer (with notice)

7. Data Retention

  • Account and service data: for the life of the account, then deleted or anonymised within about 30 days after deletion (unless law requires longer retention)
  • Usage logs: typically up to 12 months
  • Application Insights / Log Analytics telemetry: 30 days (workspace default)
  • Support records: typically up to 3 years

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, or export of your personal data, and object to certain processing. Contact us at the email below to exercise these rights.

9. Cookies

For this PoC we use cookies and similar technologies primarily for authentication and session continuity. You can control cookies through your browser settings; blocking them may prevent sign-in.

10. International Data Transfers

The Service is hosted in Australia. If you access it from elsewhere, your data may be transferred to and processed in Australia. We rely on appropriate contractual and technical safeguards with sub-processors where required.

11. Children’s Privacy

The Service is not intended for users under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance where permitted by law.
